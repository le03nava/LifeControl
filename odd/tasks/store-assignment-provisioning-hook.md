**L2** — *2026-10-17, the measurement that classified the five, and the two decisions it forced.*

Every gap was read against the code on `main @ b8b82a6`, not against the record's prose:

- **G38 is real and it is the only implementable one.** `grep` over `src/main` outside `provisioning/` finds `AccessProvisioningProducer` at exactly two call sites — `ContractService.createContract:203` and `EmployeeService.updateEmployee:252` — and **none** in `EmployeeStoreAssignmentService`, whose two writers (`createAssignment`, `closeAssignment`) save their row and return. So an assignment change moves the derivation `StoreScopeDerivation` computes and nothing enqueues a convergence: the five `company_*` claims keep the old scope until another fact enqueues a task.
- **G40's defect is already closed, and by another unit.** `KeycloakIdentityProvider.getUserRoles(userId, clientId)` catches `WebApplicationException | ProcessingException` (`:306-319`) and `toDomainFailure` (`:516-528`) maps `NotFoundException` → not-found, a `ClientErrorException` with status **409** → conflict, and **everything else** → `IdentityProviderConnectionException` → **503**. The `jakarta.ws.rs.ForbiddenException` that the 2026-10-16 probe found escaping unmapped on this gap's own path is a `ClientErrorException` subclass, so it is now translated rather than falling through `GlobalExceptionHandler`'s catch-all: the **500** the gap's own row records as the measured cost no longer happens. The fix is the G11 unit (`1588261`, merged by PR #347 as `3a19856`). What remains of G40 is the **design coupling** — the HR write reads the identity provider live, which is T67's requirement, not a defect — so G40 needs no source line and its row needs the correction.
- **G39's residual is narrower than a task.** W5c's S3 (`954e6a8`, merged by PR #294 as `bce43b8f`) already added `SUPERSEDED`, its four edges and `supersede`, and the producer now **asks first** and suppresses a same-kind open task. What the record still calls "the race residual" is (a) the same-kind case, which is genuinely already represented by the open task, and (b) the true race — two facts for one employee at the same instant — which the partial unique index `uq_access_provisioning_tasks_open` refuses and which rolls the fact back. Neither is an implementation task.
- **G42 and G43 are by-design limits whose closure is new scope.** G42's queue is the decision set on purpose (T94 keeps the operator surface per employee in the Access section) and its `null` name is T93's declared fallback; closing either means building the company-wide monitor T94 refused or a second name-resolution design. G43 needs a bounce-handling channel (an SMTP DSN reader or a Keycloak event listener), which is its own unit.
- **The hazard the hook would introduce was measured before it was written, and it forced the first decision.** `EmployeeStoreAssignmentService.createAssignment` and `closeAssignment` carry **no employee-status guard** — they resolve company, employee and store and nothing else — and `EmployeeStoreAssignmentController` exposes both to `lc-admin`/`lc-employee` (`@PreAuthorize` at `:66`, `:89`), so an assignment can be created or closed for a `Terminated` employee. `AccessProvisioningProducer.enqueue` **supersedes an open task of a different kind** before it enqueues. A naive `RECONCILE` for that employee would therefore supersede an open `DEACTIVATE`, and the account would stay `enabled` — the convergence `RECONCILE` runs (`AccessProvisioningWorkerHandler.converge:142-146`) touches roles, membership and `ensureAccountEnabled`, and `ensureAccountEnabled` **no-ops for a terminated employee** (`AccessProvisioningAccountService:184-187`), so nothing in the `RECONCILE` path disables the account. That is the shape the user chose against in **S2**.
